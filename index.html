One companion for everyday care

Keep the details.
Make room for the good stuff.

A practical notebook for pet owners, with space for both the necessary and the lovely.

01 / Care

A clearer daily routine

Keep pet profiles, health notes, weight in kg or lb, and care reminders together. Add local caregiver labels to make records easier to follow.

02 / Keep track

The useful little details

Record food purchases, pet expenses, and insurance information. Look back at the everyday details without relying on memory.

03 / Remember

More than a care log

Save photo and video diary entries, create a share card from a favorite photo, and export records or a full backup when you choose.

Your everyday records stay on your device.

Version 1.0 has no app account, cloud sync, advertising, tracking, analytics, or subscriptions. Optional feedback is sent only when you tap Send. Exports and sharing go to the services you choose. See what is stored and what is sent.

有毛要事 · 每一件小事，都值得好好记下

把琐碎记好，
把时间留给陪伴。

从一顿饭、一次称重，到一张舍不得删的照片。有毛要事，为毛孩子的日常照护留一个安静、妥帖的位置。

1.0 版本 · 免费 · 支持中文与英文

01 / 照护

日常安排，心里有数

集中记录宠物档案、健康情况、体重与照护提醒。体重支持公斤和磅，本机照护者标签让每条记录更清楚。

02 / 记录

零零碎碎，也能理清

记录宠粮购买、日常支出和保险信息。需要回看时，不必再从记忆里翻找。

03 / 陪伴

不只是一本照护记录

用照片与视频保存日记，把喜欢的照片做成分享卡片；也可以自行导出记录或完整备份。

日常记录保存在你的设备上。

1.0 版本无需应用账号，不提供云同步，也没有广告、追踪、分析统计或订阅。只有你主动点击“发送”，意见反馈才会上传；导出和分享的去向由你选择。了解数据如何保存与发送。

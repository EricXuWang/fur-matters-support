Your records, thoughtfully handled

Privacy policy

Pet care is personal. Here is what stays on your device, what you can choose to send, and how to ask for help.

Applies to Fur Matters / 有毛要事 version 1.0 and this support website.
Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Operator: Xu Wang

On this pageOn your deviceOptional feedbackExports & sharingRetention & deletionService providers中文隐私政策

The essentials. Everyday pet records are stored locally. Version 1.0 has no app accounts, cloud sync, ads, tracking, subscriptions, or analytics. Sending feedback is optional and transmits the information described below. Your chosen exports and shares may also leave your device.

1. Information on your device

The app stores pet profiles, health records, weight entries and unit preferences (kg or lb), food purchases, expense ledgers, insurance information, reminders, photo and video diaries, and caregiver labels on your device. Caregiver labels are local record labels; they do not create accounts or invite another person.

We do not automatically receive these records. Unsent feedback drafts and selected draft screenshots are also stored locally. Your operating system’s device backup settings may separately determine whether app data is included in a device backup.

Photo or video selection and notification permissions support the features you choose to use. You can manage available permissions in system Settings. The app does not upload your photo library for routine pet care.

2. Feedback you choose to send

When you explicitly tap Send in the feedback screen, the app sends:

  • Your feedback text and category (idea, bug, or other).
  • Your email address, if you enter one, so we can reply.
  • Up to two screenshots you select, including their file names and file metadata. Review images and remove private information before sending.
  • A randomly generated installation UUID and a request UUID, used to manage submissions, avoid duplicates, and limit abuse.
  • The app version, operating system version, platform, and app language, to help understand technical problems.

We use this information to respond to support requests, investigate problems, and keep the feedback service secure and reliable. The installation identifier is not an advertising identifier. These records can be associated with one another and with the email address you provide.

Feedback records and screenshots are stored through Supabase. The configured database and storage region is Canada Central. Screenshots are held in a private storage bucket. Resend delivers an email alert to the developer’s Gmail inbox with the message, optional email address, receipt, technical metadata, and screenshot count. Screenshot files are not attached to that alert.

Service providers may process IP addresses and request or delivery logs to operate and secure their services. Their processing, support operations, and email services may involve countries outside Canada. The database region is not a promise that every related processing activity occurs only in Canada.

3. Backups, exports, sharing, and external links

You can choose to export a backup or report, save or share a diary card, or send content through the system share sheet. The selected content then goes to the destination or service you choose and is subject to that service’s privacy practices. Keep backups and exported files in a place you trust; an app access lock does not encrypt a backup file.

Opening an external shop link takes you to another website or app. That service handles its own requests, browsing activity, and any information you provide under its own policies. Fur Matters does not process purchases on those external services.

4. Device authentication and security

If you enable the privacy lock, Face ID or other supported system authentication is handled by your device. The app receives the authentication result, not your face image, fingerprint, or biometric template. This is an access lock, not a separate encryption system for backups or exported records.

Feedback is transmitted over HTTPS, and screenshot storage is private rather than publicly readable. These safeguards do not constitute end-to-end encryption or guarantee that any storage or transmission is risk-free.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

You control local records through the app. Clear all local data removes local app data and the local feedback draft. It does not withdraw feedback already sent or delete copies already stored in the feedback service or email. Exports, shared copies, and operating system backups must be managed at their respective destinations.

We retain submitted feedback and related correspondence only as needed to handle support, maintain security, and meet applicable legal obligations. Version 1.0 does not implement an automatic deletion schedule for submitted feedback; we do not promise deletion after a fixed number of days.

To request access, correction, or deletion of submitted feedback, contact allen.ong.curry@gmail.com. Include a receipt ID or the email address used in the submission so we can locate it. We may ask for limited information to verify the request. If a legal or security reason prevents full deletion, we will explain the remaining limitation. Requests concerning copies held by a service you chose may also need to be directed to that service.

6. No advertising or tracking

Version 1.0 does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, sell personal information, or use feedback for advertising or cross-app tracking. It has no app account system, cloud synchronization, or subscriptions.

7. This website and TestFlight

This static support website has no analytics scripts, sign-up forms, or cookies added by us. GitHub Pages hosts it; GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address and request logs when you visit.

If you test the app with TestFlight, Apple separately processes testing information and may make test feedback, crash reports, or diagnostics available to the developer as described in Apple’s TestFlight & Privacy notice. That process is separate from the app’s optional feedback feature.

8. Audience and policy updates

Fur Matters is a pet-owner utility, not an app in Apple’s Kids Category. We do not intentionally seek children’s personal information. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information about anyone in feedback.

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the current policy. For questions about this policy, contact the operator, Xu Wang, at allen.ong.curry@gmail.com.

9. Provider privacy information

  • Supabase privacy policy — feedback database, storage, and backend services
  • Resend privacy policy — feedback email delivery
  • Google privacy policy — the developer’s Gmail support mailbox
  • GitHub privacy statement — this support website
  • Apple privacy information — device services and TestFlight as applicable

隐私政策

适用于有毛要事 / Fur Matters 1.0 版本及本支持网站。
更新日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日 · 运营者：Xu Wang

先说重点。日常宠物记录保存在本机。1.0 版本没有应用账号、云同步、广告、追踪、订阅或分析统计。意见反馈仅在你主动发送时上传下文列出的信息；你选择的导出和分享也可能使内容离开设备。

1. 保存在设备上的信息

应用在本机保存宠物档案、健康记录、体重与单位偏好（公斤／磅）、宠粮购买、支出账本、保险信息、提醒、照片与视频日记以及照护者标签。照护者标签仅用于本机记录，不会创建账号或邀请他人。

我们不会自动接收这些记录。未发送的反馈草稿及草稿中选择的截图也保存在本机。操作系统的设备备份设置，可能另行决定是否将应用数据纳入设备备份。

照片或视频选择、通知权限用于你主动使用的相应功能。你可以在系统设置中管理可用权限。日常照护功能不会上传你的照片图库。

2. 你主动发送的意见反馈

只有当你在反馈页面明确点击“发送”时，应用才会发送以下信息：

  • 反馈文字与分类（想法、问题或其他）。
  • 你自愿填写的邮箱，用于回复。
  • 你选择的最多两张截图，以及文件名和文件元数据。发送前请检查并去除图片中的私密信息。
  • 随机生成的安装 UUID 与请求 UUID，用于管理提交、避免重复和限制滥用。
  • 应用版本、操作系统版本、平台与应用语言，用于了解技术问题。

我们将这些信息用于处理支持请求、排查问题，以及保障反馈服务的安全与可靠。安装标识符不是广告标识符。这些信息可能相互关联，也可能与你填写的邮箱关联。

反馈记录与截图通过 Supabase 保存，已配置的数据库与存储区域为加拿大中部（Canada Central）。截图存放于私有存储桶。Resend 向开发者的 Gmail 邮箱发送提醒邮件，包含反馈内容、可选邮箱、回执、技术元数据和截图数量，不附带截图文件。

服务提供商可能为运行和保护服务而处理 IP 地址、请求日志或投递日志。其处理、支持和邮件服务可能涉及加拿大以外的国家或地区。数据库所在区域不代表所有相关处理都仅在加拿大进行。

3. 备份、导出、分享与外部链接

你可以自行导出备份或报告、保存或分享日记卡片，或通过系统分享菜单发送内容。所选内容会传输到你选择的目标或服务，并适用该服务的隐私规则。请将备份与导出文件保存在可信的位置；应用访问锁不会加密备份文件。

打开外部商店链接会进入其他网站或应用。该服务依其规则处理请求、浏览活动及你提供的信息。有毛要事不处理这些外部服务中的购买交易。

4. 设备身份验证与安全

启用隐私锁后，Face ID 或其他受支持的系统验证由设备完成。应用仅接收验证结果，不会获得面部图像、指纹或生物识别模板。隐私锁是访问限制，不是针对备份或导出记录的独立加密系统。

反馈通过 HTTPS 传输，截图存储为私有而非公开可读。这些措施不等于端到端加密，也不代表任何存储或传输都不存在风险。

5. 保存期限、删除与选择

你可以在应用内管理本地记录。“清除全部本地数据”会删除本机应用数据和本地反馈草稿，但不会撤回已提交的反馈，也不会删除反馈服务或邮件中的已有副本。已导出、分享或纳入系统备份的副本，需要在相应位置另行管理。

已提交的反馈及相关往来仅保留至支持处理、安全保障和适用法律要求所必要的时间。1.0 版本没有针对已提交反馈的自动删除计划，我们不承诺在固定天数后自动删除。

如需访问、更正或删除已提交反馈，请联系 allen.ong.curry@gmail.com，并提供回执编号或提交时填写的邮箱，以便定位记录。我们可能要求少量信息核验请求。如因法律或安全原因无法完全删除，会说明剩余限制。对于你自行选择的其他服务所保存的副本，可能需要同时联系该服务。

6. 无广告与追踪

1.0 版本不包含广告或分析统计 SDK，不出售个人信息，也不将反馈用于广告或跨应用追踪。应用没有账号系统、云同步或订阅。

7. 本网站与 TestFlight

本静态支持网站没有分析统计脚本、注册表单或由我们添加的 Cookie。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管；访问时，GitHub 可能接收 IP 地址、请求日志等技术信息。

如通过 TestFlight 测试应用，Apple 会另行处理测试信息，并可能依照 Apple 的 TestFlight 与隐私说明向开发者提供测试反馈、崩溃报告或诊断信息。这与应用内可选的意见反馈功能是不同流程。

8. 适用人群与政策更新

有毛要事是面向宠物照护者的工具，不属于 Apple 的儿童类别应用。我们不会有意索取儿童的个人信息。请勿在反馈中包含与问题无关的任何人的个人信息。

应用功能或数据处理方式变化时，本政策可能更新，当前版本以页首日期为准。政策相关问题请联系运营者 Xu Wang：allen.ong.curry@gmail.com。

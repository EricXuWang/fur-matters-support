Getting started

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. Version 1.0 is free and has no app accounts or subscriptions. Pet records are stored on your device; there is no cloud synchronization. Local caregiver labels help organize records but do not share them with another device.

Can I use English, Chinese, kilograms, or pounds?

Open More → 语言 / Language to choose English or 简体中文. When adding or editing a health record, use Weight unit to choose kg or lb. Switching units converts the weight entered in that editor; saving the record remembers your choice for future records. The health journal’s weight chart has a separate Chart weight unit control that changes the chart display without changing saved weights.

Is health information a veterinary diagnosis?

No. Fur Matters organizes information you record and may provide general care information. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace advice from a qualified veterinarian. For health concerns or urgent symptoms, contact your veterinarian.

Reminders

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check that notifications are allowed for Fur Matters in system Settings, the reminder is enabled, and its time is still in the future. Focus modes and other system settings can affect delivery.

The app schedules a rolling queue of up to 60 upcoming care reminders within the next 90 days. Reopen the app regularly to refresh the queue, especially after changing dates, time zone, or notification settings. A large number of reminders can fill that queue earlier; the app’s coverage information shows the current limit.

Will reminders keep renewing without opening the app?

Do not rely on an unlimited background schedule. Open the app to renew notification coverage when prompted and periodically while using recurring reminders. Keep a separate plan for time-critical care.

Backups & your records

How do I prepare for a new phone or reinstall?

Before changing phones, deleting the app, or installing a test build, open More → Backup & transfer → Export full backup and save the file somewhere you can access from the new device. Check that the file was saved and keep the original until you have verified the restored records and attachments.

A report is useful for reading or sharing selected records; it is not a replacement for a complete backup. The app does not provide cloud sync or a developer-held recovery copy of everyday records.

Does the privacy lock encrypt my backup?

No. The privacy lock uses supported device authentication to restrict opening the app. It does not encrypt exported backups or shared files. Store those files securely and choose their recipients carefully.

What does “Clear all local data” remove?

It removes local app records and the local feedback draft. Exported files, copies you shared, operating system backups, and feedback already submitted to the developer remain separate. Export a backup first if you need to keep your records.

Photos & sharing

Why can’t I find a photo or video?

Use the system picker to select the media you want to add. If access is limited or denied, review the app’s photo permissions in system Settings and make the desired items available. A photo diary and its attachments are stored locally unless you choose to export or share them.

How do I make a diary share card?

Open a diary entry and choose its share-card option. Select a photo, adjust the available card options, and check the preview before exporting. A card uses a still photo; a video diary attachment does not become a video share card. Use the system share sheet to choose where the exported card goes.

If a photo is missing in the preview, reselect an available photo and try again. If export fails, keep the diary entry, reopen the app, and retry. Include your app and iOS versions when reporting a persistent problem.

Feedback & privacy

What should I include in a bug report?

Open More → Feedback or email allen.ong.curry@gmail.com. Describe what you tried, what you expected, and what happened. In-app feedback can include up to two selected screenshots and an optional reply email. Remove private information from images first.

In-app feedback also sends random installation and request identifiers, app and operating system versions, platform, and language when you tap Send. Keep the submission receipt if one is shown. See the privacy policy for details.

My feedback was not sent. What can I do?

Check your connection and retry from the feedback screen. If the service is temporarily unavailable or rate-limited, wait before retrying. You can also email support. A sent receipt confirms that the feedback service accepted the submission; it does not promise an immediate reply.

How do I request deletion of submitted feedback?

Email allen.ong.curry@gmail.com with the receipt ID or the email used in your submission. Clearing local data does not delete submitted feedback or email copies. We will handle the request subject to the support, security, and legal limits described in our retention and deletion policy.

开始使用

需要注册账号或订阅吗？

不需要。1.0 版本免费，没有应用账号或订阅。宠物记录保存在当前设备，不提供云同步。照护者标签仅用于整理本机记录，不会与另一台设备共享数据。

可以切换语言和体重单位吗？

在“我的 → 语言 / Language”中选择简体中文或 English。添加或编辑健康记录时，使用“体重单位”选择公斤（kg）或磅（lb）。切换会自动换算当前编辑页中已填写的体重，保存记录后会记住所选单位，用于之后的新记录。健康手账中的体重图表另有“图表体重单位”，仅切换图表显示，不改变已保存的体重。

健康信息可以作为兽医诊断吗？

不可以。有毛要事整理你自行记录的信息，并可能提供一般照护知识，不用于诊断、治疗，也不替代专业兽医建议。遇到健康问题或紧急症状，请联系兽医。

提醒与通知

为什么没有收到提醒？

请先在系统设置中允许有毛要事发送通知，确认提醒已启用、时间尚未过去。专注模式等系统设置也可能影响通知显示。

应用会滚动安排未来 90 天内最多 60 条即将到来的照护提醒。请定期重新打开应用以更新队列，尤其是在修改日期、时区或通知设置之后。提醒较多时，队列可能更早排满；应用中的覆盖范围信息会显示当前限制。

一直不打开应用，提醒也会自动续上吗？

请不要依赖无限期的后台排程。看到续排提示时应重新打开应用，使用重复提醒时也应定期打开。对时间要求严格的照护事项，请同时做好其他安排。

备份与数据

换手机、重装或安装测试版前，需要做什么？

先打开“我的 → 备份与换机 → 导出完整备份”，保存到新设备也能访问的位置，并确认文件已成功保存。在核对恢复后的记录与附件之前，请保留原始备份。

报告适合阅读或分享部分记录，不能替代完整备份。应用不提供云同步，开发者也没有日常记录的恢复副本。

隐私锁会加密备份吗？

不会。隐私锁使用受支持的系统验证来限制打开应用，不会加密导出的备份或分享文件。请妥善保存文件，并确认接收方。

“清除全部本地数据”会删除哪些内容？

会删除本机应用记录和本地反馈草稿。导出文件、已分享副本、系统备份，以及已经提交给开发者的反馈，需另行处理。如果需要保留记录，请先导出备份。

照片与分享卡片

找不到想用的照片或视频怎么办？

请通过系统选择器选择需要添加的内容。如果照片权限受限或被拒绝，可到系统设置检查应用权限，并允许访问需要的项目。照片日记及附件保存在本机，除非你主动导出或分享。

怎样生成日记分享卡片？

打开日记，进入分享卡片选项，选择照片并调整可用的卡片设置，检查预览后导出，再通过系统分享菜单选择去向。卡片使用静态照片，视频附件不会变成视频分享卡片。

如果预览没有照片，请重新选择一张可访问的照片再试。如果导出失败，请保留日记，重新打开应用后重试。持续出现问题时，反馈中请写明应用与 iOS 版本。

反馈与隐私

报告问题时，提供什么信息有帮助？

可打开“我的 → 意见与建议”，或写信至 allen.ong.curry@gmail.com。请说明操作步骤、预期结果与实际情况。应用内反馈可附最多两张自选截图，回复邮箱为选填；发送前请去除截图中的私密信息。

点击“发送”时，应用还会发送随机安装与请求标识符、应用和系统版本、平台及语言。如显示提交回执，请保留。详见隐私政策。

反馈没有发出去怎么办？

请检查网络后在反馈页面重试。如果服务暂时不可用或提交过于频繁，请稍后重试，也可直接邮件联系。提交回执表示反馈服务已接收内容，不代表一定会立即收到人工回复。

如何删除已经提交的反馈？

请写信至 allen.ong.curry@gmail.com，提供回执编号或提交时使用的邮箱。清除本地数据不会删除已提交反馈或邮件副本。我们将依隐私政策所述的支持、安全及法律要求处理请求。
